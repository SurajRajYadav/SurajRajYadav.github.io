01About

I'm a graduate of Coventry University (BSc Hons, Ethical Hacking and Cyber Security, at Softwarica College in Kathmandu), where I ranked first in my cohort every year and graduated with First Class Honours with Distinction.

02Research— interests & experience

What I want to work on.

I want to understand the internal behavior of advanced AI systems and evaluate them honestly. My papers ask where behavior comes from inside a model, whether benchmark scores reflect capability, and when added components such as retrieval or verification really help.

A thread through my work is auditing my own results. Several of my papers report negative or cautionary findings, and one audits its own headline numbers.

03Papers— all four under review, with code
PAPER_01Under review

Retrieval Helps Far More Than It Hurts, but Simple Gates Cannot Recover the Rest

A paired failure analysis of retrieval-augmented multi-hop QA with a small language model.

On 500 HotpotQA questions, retrieval raised soft accuracy from 14.2% to 34.0%. It helped 122 questions and hurt 23. Three "when to retrieve" gates did no better than always retrieving.

Suraj Raj Yadav*, Ganesh Bhusal†, Shiva Maharjan†

RAG / Multi-hop QA / Failure analysis

PAPER_02Under review

Do Benchmark Scores Survive Answer-Preserving Perturbations?

A small-scale robustness and contamination-signal study on MMLU and GSM8K.

Reordering MMLU options moved the 1.5B model's accuracy by only −0.1 points, but adding one irrelevant sentence cost Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct 11.0 points on GSM8K. No sign that scores are inflated by contamination.

Suraj Raj Yadav*, Ganesh Bhusal†, Shiva Maharjan†

Benchmarks / Robustness / Contamination

Paper ↗ PDF ↓ Code ↗
PAPER_03Under review

How Does a Small Transformer Copy a Counterfactual Fact from Context Instead of Recalling It?

Patching, attribution and ablation in GPT-2 small.

GPT-2 small copies the context answer in about 99% of prompts that restate the question. Copying is spread across 16 to 24 late-layer heads rather than a handful: knocking out the top 24 removes 0.97 of the logit difference.

Suraj Raj Yadav*, Ganesh Bhusal, Shiva Maharjan

Activation patching / Attribution / Ablation

Paper ↗ PDF ↓ Code ↗
PAPER_04Under review

Evidence-Span Verification for Quantitative Claim Extraction from Materials-Science Abstracts

A cautionary case study.

Requiring the model to quote its evidence pushed precision to about 1.0 but cut recall from 0.97 to 0.25. The paper then audits its own headline numbers, finding annotation anchoring and a failed fine-tune, and gives a protocol for sound re-evaluation.

Suraj Raj Yadav*, Ganesh Bhusal, Shiva Maharjan
